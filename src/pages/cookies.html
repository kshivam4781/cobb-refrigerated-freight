1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website places in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, let a site remember information on your device. They can be set by the site you are visiting (“first-party”) or by other companies whose content appears on it (“third-party”).

4. Your choices

  • Our cookie notice: choose “Essential only” or “Accept all”. You can change your mind at any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.
  • Privacy signals: if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, we automatically use “Essential only”.
  • Your browser: you can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. The site will still work; you may just see the cookie notice again.
