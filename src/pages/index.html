03The cold chain, handled

How we protect every load

Perishables don’t get second chances. Here’s the routine behind every reefer load we run.

  1. Confirm the requirements

    Set point, commodity, pickup windows, and delivery appointments are locked in before a truck is dispatched.

  2. Pre-cool the trailer

    The reefer is brought down to temperature before loading, so product never sits in a warm box.

  3. Load, verify, and seal

    Product temperature and count are checked at the dock, and the load is sealed for the road.

  4. Hold temp in transit

    Our drivers keep the unit running and checked from pickup to delivery — with our own shop and roadside truck as backup.

  5. Deliver at spec

    Product arrives on time and in condition. Need storage or a cross-dock along the way? We handle that too.

07FAQ

Questions shippers ask

Don’t see yours? Call (209) 576-0327 and talk to the people who actually run the trucks.

Where do you haul?

We’re based in Modesto and hold interstate operating authority (MC-147824), so we can run loads across California and out of state. Tell us your lanes and we’ll let you know how we can help.

What kinds of loads can you carry?

Our fleet is set up for refrigerated food, beverages, paper products, dry bulk commodities, and general freight — frozen, chilled, or dry.

Can I use your storage or cross-dock without your trucking?

Ask us. Storage and cross-dock can be arranged on their own or combined with our transportation — combining them usually saves a step and a hand-off.

Is your repair shop open to outside trucks?

Yes. Owner-operators and fleets can call or send a request to book maintenance or repairs on tractors, trailers, and reefer units.

How do I get roadside help?

Call (209) 576-0327 with your location, unit and trailer number, and a description of the problem. We’ll tell you what we can fix on site and get a truck headed your way.

How do I verify your authority and insurance?

Look us up on the FMCSA SAFER system with USDOT 145920. We’re happy to send a certificate of insurance on request.
