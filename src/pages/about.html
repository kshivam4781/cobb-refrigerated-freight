About Cobb

A Modesto, California refrigerated carrier keeping the Central Valley’s freight cold, on time, and on the road.

Who we are

Rooted in the Central Valley

Cobb Refrigerated Freight Inc operates from our yard at 420 California Ave in Modesto — in the middle of one of the most productive farming regions in the world.

We’ve held a federal safety rating on record with the FMCSA since 1989. Today we run a focused fleet of 13 power units and 15 professional drivers, logging more than 775,000 miles in 2025 alone.

Our size is our advantage: you get the attention of a local company, the reliability of an asset-based carrier, and the convenience of storage, cross-dock, and an in-house repair shop — all at one address.
