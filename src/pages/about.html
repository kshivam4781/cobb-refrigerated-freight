01Our story

Small enough to answer the phone. Equipped to do the whole job.

Cobb Refrigerated Freight Inc runs from our yard at 420 California Ave in Modesto — in the middle of one of the most productive farming regions in the world.

We’ve held a federal safety rating on record with the FMCSA since 1989. Today we run a focused fleet of 13 power units and 15 professional drivers, and logged more than 775,000 miles in 2025.

Our size is the advantage: you get the attention of a local company, the reliability of an asset-based carrier, and the convenience of storage, cross-dock, and an in-house repair shop — all at one address.
