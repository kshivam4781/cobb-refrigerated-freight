Privacy Policy

How Cobb Refrigerated Freight Inc collects, uses, and protects the information you share through this website.

The short version

  • We only collect what you choose to send us through our quote and service forms.
  • We use it to answer your request and do business with you. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.
  • This website does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.
  • You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time by calling (209) 576-0327.

This Privacy Policy applies to the website operated by Cobb Refrigerated Freight Inc (“Cobb,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), 420 California Ave, Modesto, CA 95351 (USDOT 145920, MC-147824). It does not cover information we handle offline, such as shipping documents, bills of lading, or repair orders, which are governed by the agreements that apply to those services.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you request a quote, storage, or shop service, we collect the details you enter, which may include:

  • Contact details: name, company, phone number, and email address.
  • Request details: service needed, pickup and delivery locations, commodity, weight, temperature requirements, number of pallets, dates, truck or trailer type, the problem you are having, the vehicle’s location, and any other notes you add.

Please do not include sensitive information such as Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, or health information in our forms.

Information collected automatically

We do not run analytics or tracking software. Our website host, GitHub Pages, may automatically record technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, for security and to operate the service. When you send a form, the address of the page you sent it from is included with your request.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to your request, prepare quotes, and schedule transportation, storage, cross-dock, repair, or roadside service.
  • To communicate with you about your request or our services.
  • To keep business records, prevent fraud and spam, and protect the security of our website.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers that help us run this website, bound to use the information only to provide their services to us: our web host (GitHub), our form-delivery providers (Web3Forms, with FormSubmit as a backup), and our email provider.
  • Google Maps, only if you choose to load the embedded map. Google then receives information such as your IP address and may set cookies under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal and safety reasons: when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Cobb, our customers, or others.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, sale, or transfer of all or part of our business.

4. Cookies & local storage

This website does not set its own cookies. It saves one small setting in your browser’s local storage to remember your cookie choice. Embedded Google Maps may set third-party cookies, so maps load only if you allow them. See our Cookie Policy for details.

5. Retention & security

We keep the information you send us for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, provide our services, and meet our legal and business record-keeping needs, and then delete it. Copies of form submissions held by our form-delivery provider are deleted automatically after no more than three years.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, and this website is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices & rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information you have sent us, correct it, or delete it. Call (209) 576-0327 or write to us at the address below. We will verify your request by confirming details you provided, and respond within the time required by law. You will not be treated differently for making a request.

Residents of states with consumer privacy laws (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and others) may have additional rights where those laws apply to us, including the right to know, access, correct, delete, and opt out of the sale of personal information, targeted advertising, or profiling. Because we do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or profile you, there is nothing to opt out of, but you may still contact us with any request. If we deny a request, you may appeal by contacting us and asking us to reconsider.

7. California residents

This section supplements the policy above for California residents under the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA), and California’s “Shine the Light” law, to the extent each applies to us.

  • Categories collected in the last 12 months: identifiers (name, email, phone, IP address); commercial information (details of the services you request); and internet activity limited to technical server logs. Sources: you, and automatically from your browser.
  • Purposes: the business purposes described in Section 2.
  • Disclosures: to the service providers listed in Section 3 for business purposes. We have not sold or shared personal information, and we do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.
  • Sensitive personal information: we do not intentionally collect it and do not use it to infer characteristics about you.
  • Your rights: to know, access, correct, and delete your personal information, and to be free from discrimination for exercising these rights. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof of the agent’s authority and to verify your identity.
  • Shine the Light: we do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct-marketing purposes.

8. Do Not Track & Global Privacy Control

We do not track visitors across other websites. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) or Do Not Track signal, we treat it as a choice to turn off optional third-party content, so embedded maps will not load unless you ask for them.

9. Children’s privacy

This website is intended for businesses and adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version here and update the effective date above. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.
