Storage

Short-term and staging storage for temperature-sensitive and dry freight, right at our Modesto yard, with a direct hand-off to our trucks when it’s time to ship.

  • Temperature-controlled storage
  • Short-term & staging storage
  • Trailer storage & drop yard
  • Palletized product handling

Cross Dock

Move product from inbound to outbound trailers quickly. Less dwell time, less handling, lower storage costs, and the cold chain stays intact.

  • Trailer-to-trailer transfers
  • Load consolidation
  • Load splitting & distribution
  • Temperature-protected handling
  • Reduced dwell time
  • Coordinated with our fleet
